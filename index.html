    How to play

    Escape moves first. Select one of your pieces, then tap a destination on the board or choose a move from the list. Each turn uses one action.

    Corridors and Jail
    Guards and Associates move one space at a time. The Prisoner can move one or two spaces in a straight line. Both spaces must be clear for a two-space move. Walls block movement in both directions. Leave the Jail onto any empty space immediately north, east, south, or west. Leaving takes one turn, and the Prisoner cannot reenter.
    Push and displacement
    Guards and Associates can push one another into an empty junction directly behind them. A pushed piece cannot return to its old junction on its next turn.
    Sacrifice
    The Prisoner can remove an adjacent Associate and take its place. Confirm this action before moving.
    Capture
    A Guard can move into the Prisoner’s junction to win. The Prisoner is safe inside Jail.
    Escape gates
    Move the Prisoner onto the corridor space directly inside one of the four perimeter gates, then use another turn to exit. An Associate directly behind the Prisoner can push them out to win. Opposing pieces can push each other out through a gate. The Prisoner can push an Associate out and take the space.
    Secret tunnels
    Revealed when the Prisoner stands on an entrance. On a later turn, the Prisoner may use the tunnel if the paired junction is empty. The connection hides when the Prisoner leaves the tunnel junctions.
    Draw
    Pass only if you have no legal action. The match draws after two consecutive passes or 60 total turns without a winner.
